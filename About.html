Corporate Roots

From Local Vision to Global Conglomerate

Armana Group started its journey in 1995 with a modest yet powerful vision: to transform Bangladesh’s textile landscape through precision engineering, uncompromised quality control, and deep respect for human labor.

Evolution Timeline

Milestones That Defined Our Journey

Key developmental phases shaping Armana Group into an internationally accredited industrial titan.

Phase I

1995 – 2002

Establishment of foundational apparel manufacturing plants in Dhaka, initiating direct supply lines for regional markets.

Phase II

2003 – 2012

Rapid expansion into automated denim production lines, securing direct tier-1 vendor status with major European and US retailers.

Phase III

2013 – 2020

Deployment of green-factory initiatives, zero-discharge effluent treatment plants, and advanced laser washing technology.

Phase IV

2021 – Present

Full digital transformation, AI-driven supply chain tracking, and expansion into sustainable high-performance denim.

Core Ethos

Guiding Principles & Corporate Values

At Armana Group, sustainable manufacturing is not just compliance—it is our core operating culture. We balance high-speed output with absolute environmental stewardship.

Integrity & Compliance

Strict adherence to international labor standards, fair wages, safe working environments, and ZDHC chemical management guidelines.

Technological Mastery

Continuous reinvestment in cutting-edge machinery, automated stitching units, computerised pattern grading, and smart warehousing.

Environmental Stewardship

Pioneering water-recycling systems, solar energy integration, and ozone-washing technologies to reduce our carbon footprint.

Executive Oversight

Rigorous Quality & Executive Governance

Armana Group is led by an executive board with decades of combined textile engineering expertise. Every production stage is overseen by dedicated quality control auditors who inspect raw fiber integrity through finished garment packaging.
